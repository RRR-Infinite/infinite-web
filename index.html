About us

RRR Infinite Turf is a floodlit artificial-turf ground on Water Canal Road in Korattur, Chennai, built for football and cricket. The pitch is enclosed with high safety netting and lit end to end, and the gates stay open around the clock.

  • SportsFootball & cricket
  • SurfaceArtificial turf, fully netted
  • Open24 hours, every day

Call +91 98402 20196 to reserve a slot, or drop by and we will fit you in.
